Lupen Audio Pack v4 - Low Tone Extended
Original procedural WAV assets regenerated from v3 direction with longer hit sounds and a dedicated enemy ship explosion.

Main updates:
- Added lupen_enemy_ship_destroyed_low_explosion.wav for enemy ship destruction.
- Extended successful hit sounds so they feel more satisfying and less clipped.
- Kept the lower-tone direction: less high pitch, more rumble, thud and low-mid weight.

Recommended mapping:
- lupen_jump_hyper_arrive_low_extended.wav        -> jump complete / arrive at new node
- lupen_planet_land_low_heavy_extended.wav        -> landing on planet
- lupen_ship_destroyed_low_boom_extended.wav      -> larger/player ship destroyed
- lupen_enemy_ship_destroyed_low_explosion.wav    -> enemy ship destroyed
- lupen_laser_light_low_plasma_extended.wav       -> standard/light laser pulse
- lupen_laser_heavy_low_cannon_extended.wav       -> heavy weapon / heavier laser
- lupen_kinetic_railgun_low_extended.wav          -> future kinetic/rail weapon variant
- lupen_shield_hit_low_extended.wav               -> successful hit on shield
- lupen_hull_hit_low_metal_extended.wav           -> successful hit on hull
- lupen_weapon_hit_marker_low_extended.wav        -> repeated enemy hit/impact marker
- lupen_ui_confirm_low_click.wav                  -> button confirm
- lupen_ui_deny_low_click.wav                     -> unavailable/error
- lupen_reward_claim_low_dark_extended.wav        -> reward/bounty claim

Format:
- WAV
- mono
- 44.1 kHz
- 16-bit PCM

Suggested in-game volumes:
- repeated hit marker: 0.18-0.32
- shield/hull hits: 0.25-0.45
- weapons: 0.25-0.42
- enemy explosion: 0.42-0.58
- large explosion: 0.50-0.70
- jump/land: 0.38-0.58
- UI: 0.10-0.22
