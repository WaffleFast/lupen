# Player progression route

Step 5 establishes one dependable route from a new profile into Chapter I: Frontier.

## Starter Pilot Programme

The guided first session teaches the immediate controls and economy:

1. Claim the Pioneer Hunter.
2. Inspect its Loadout.
3. Complete a profitable Iron trade.
4. Buy and fit the Hunter's second Pulse Laser.
5. Fit the issued Cargo Pod.
6. Accept, complete and claim a bounty.
7. Upgrade the Pulse Laser in the Forge.
8. Inspect hull condition and repair if necessary.
9. Open Journey to continue Morgan's Academy route.

The Hunter is issued with one mounted Pulse Laser, plus an unfitted Cargo Pod and Jump Drive. This makes the Store, second weapon, and first attachment meaningful without leaving the pilot unarmed.

## Academy route

Morgan tracks nine durable Academy milestones:

- Claim the starter ship.
- Launch from Asteron Prime.
- Complete a profitable trade.
- Equip two guns.
- Equip an attachment.
- Destroy three Erebus bots.
- Claim a completed bounty.
- Complete a hull repair or confirm a full-condition repair inspection.
- Purchase a second Pioneer hull.

The final hull purchase is deliberately the Frontier gate. A fresh pilot can reach the Pioneer Freighter through the first trade, bounty, and a small amount of continued play without skipping directly to the Destroyer or Moth.

## Frontier and future plans

When all Academy milestones are complete, Chapter I: Frontier becomes active automatically. Morgan then guides launch orientation, trade, resource recovery, Erebus combat and Frontier certification.

Chapter II remains locked. Its route text now makes the long-term promise explicit: completing Frontier reveals the path toward the next ship-plan family.

## Reliability rules

- Bounty claims update Journey and the Pilot Profile bounty total without granting extra bounty XP.
- Second-hull ownership is reconciled from the saved fleet as well as recorded at purchase time.
- Starter ship, fitted weapons, fitted attachments, claimed bounty and second Pioneer ownership recover correctly after save load.
- Tutorial steps skip safely when the required state is already satisfied.
