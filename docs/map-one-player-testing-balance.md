# Map 1 player-testing balance

This is the first intentional Map 1 balance baseline for player testing. It replaces temporary economy values and records the progression curve that the automated playtest protects.

## Progression targets

| Loop | Player-testing baseline | Intended result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting funds | 10,000 CR | The Hunter is ready immediately without skipping the first earned hull. |
| Daily bounties | 6,000 CR and 185 Lupen Shards total | One full bounty set contributes meaningfully toward the 24,000 CR Freighter. It can Forge one item through Level III, but not Level IV. |
| Daily trade contracts | 12,260 CR total | One full courier set contributes toward the Freighter and establishes the higher-value trade loop needed for later combat hulls. |
| All daily contracts | 18,260 CR total | Completing both daily paths puts the first earned hull within reach while leaving the Destroyer and Behemoth as substantial goals. |
| Mining | 10 Lupen Shards per depleted asteroid | Mining supplements Forge progression without matching a combat bounty. Cumulative Forge levels require 3, 10, 25 and 55 asteroid clears. |
| Combat levels | 2,500 XP per level through Level 5 | The current mixed Erebus spawn plan reaches Level 5 in about 86 kills: roughly 92 minutes at the typical automated-playtest pace. |

## Ship curve

| Ship | Price | Hull | Shield | Armor | Evasion | Cargo | Guns | Attachments |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Pioneer Hunter | 0 | 720 | 180 | 10 | 18 | 150 | 2 | 2 |
| Pioneer Freighter | 24,000 | 1,300 | 135 | 18 | 7 | 300 | 2 | 4 |
| Pioneer Destroyer | 66,000 | 1,450 | 300 | 24 | 12 | 120 | 4 | 3 |
| Pioneer Behemoth | 240,000 | 2,400 | 600 | 36 | 6 | 220 | 6 | 5 |

The Freighter doubles starter cargo and leans into equipment capacity. The Destroyer doubles the Hunter's weapon mounts and substantially improves survival. The Behemoth is the long-term flagship, with six mounts and more than three times the Hunter's combined defensive pool.

## Live-market curve

The 90-second market refresh remains unchanged. At base prices, the best per-unit margins are 12 CR for Iron, 18 CR for Copper and 28 CR for Cobalt. A full 150-unit Hunter hold therefore yields at most 1,800 CR, 2,700 CR or 4,200 CR before market movement.

Cobalt's Nyxara base price is 102 CR. This keeps it the strongest Map 1 commodity while bringing a repeatable full-hold run below the 4,920 CR high-risk daily courier reward.

## Combat baseline

Bot durability and the Pioneer weapon-mount curve are regression-protected together. The two-mount Hunter takes the longest to clear Erebus targets and carries the greatest cumulative repair risk. The four-mount Destroyer shortens fights and has a substantially larger defensive pool. The six-mount Behemoth is fastest and safest, while sustained combat without planetary repairs can still destroy a careless pilot.

PvP damage is resolved from server-known equipped weapons. Protected nodes reject player attacks; contested lower-sector nodes allow them. Server-known hull, shield, and armor values prevent the browser from supplying arbitrary PvP health totals.

## Player-test questions

- Does the first earned ship arrive at a satisfying time?
- Do combat, trade, and mining each feel worthwhile without one invalidating the others?
- Does a Forge upgrade feel earned, and is its effect noticeable?
- Is the Erebus Behemoth threatening without making the starter Hunter unusable?
- Does Level 3 arrive before Map 1 becomes repetitive?

Record completion time, chosen activity, purchases, Forge levels, deaths, remaining credits, and the point where the player first feels under- or over-powered.
